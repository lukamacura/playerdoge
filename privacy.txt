How We Protect Your Information and Your Account

Why We May Require Temporary Account Access

Our business model involves logging into your game account solely to complete your requested in-game purchases because:

We purchase official Google Play and Apple gift cards in bulk from trusted suppliers at discounted rates.

We redeem these cards on our Google or Apple accounts and use the Coins to buy your requested packs directly inside your game, the same way you would yourself.

This ensures game developers receive the full listed price for every transaction, appearing exactly like any other player purchase.

We do not:

Use stolen payment methods.

Use bots or automated gameplay.

Access your account for any purpose beyond completing your requested purchase.

Security Practices During Account Access

Credentials stored with encryption in transit and at rest.

Access by trained buyer agents - never bots - to mimic normal player behavior and avoid triggering automated detection systems.

Vetting of each game and order before acceptance.

Agents log in, complete the purchase, and log out - nothing else is accessed or altered.

Credentials are never shared outside our authorized fulfillment team.

All employees and contractors with access to personal data are required to complete privacy and data protection training, and are bound by confidentiality agreements.

Why This Is Low Risk Under Game Terms of Service

Game ToS often prohibit account sharing to stop account sales, hacking, or botting - not legitimate purchasing.

Players regularly log in from new devices or share with trusted friends without penalties.

Our process mirrors normal player activity, uses official payment channels, and benefits developers by increasing engagement and revenue.
